Why do 73% of enterprise AI projects never reach production?

Enterprise teams generate hundreds of AI ideas. Without a structured evaluation framework, resources get committed to the wrong initiatives — ones with low strategic alignment, poor data readiness, mismatched platforms, or governance gaps discovered only after significant investment. AI Catalyst replaces opinion-driven prioritisation with a disciplined, reproducible process — from first idea to investment committee verdict.

End-to-end AI investment evaluation

Nine integrated capabilities — from multi-enterprise workspace management to a live 4-persona Executive Review Panel and a 13-tool AI token economics control framework — each mapped to a published framework or governance standard.

Multi-Enterprise Workspaces

Manage multiple enterprises in one platform. Each workspace has its own enterprise profile (tech stack, Google Workspace licensing, compliance, AI budget), department LOB profiles, and scoped use case portfolio.

BXT Gate Evaluation

Every idea passes a Business × Experience × Technology gate — aligned to Google's AI pre-filter. Only viable use cases advance to scoring, preventing wasted evaluation effort on weak ideas.

4-Framework Scoring Engine

Deterministic scoring across Google Cloud Adoption Framework for AI (0–100), McKinsey quadrant (Quick Win / Strategic Bet / Foundation / Defer), MIT Sloan readiness (Data / Talent / Strategy), and Gartner AI maturity gap — all from your enterprise profile, zero extra AI calls.

Org-Specific ROI Engine

Calculates 24-month ROI, payback period, and 3-year NPV from your actual staffing costs and process volumes. Monte Carlo simulation produces conservative / expected / optimistic ranges with probability of positive ROI.

GADF Sequential Gate Advisory

Google AI Decision Framework — three sequential gates (Workspace/Gemini sufficiency → Low-code viability → Build necessity) determine the right platform tier. Start Simple Mandate ensures Phase 1 always begins at the simplest viable tier.

Executive Review Panel

Simulates a real enterprise approval process: Business Sponsor, Risk & Assurance Officer, Chief Information Officer, and Committee Chair each deliberate in their authentic voice. Live streaming deliberation with canvas-style decision artifact. GADF compliance gates the verdict.

Governance & Responsible AI

Auto-generates a tailored governance checklist aligned to Google Cloud's responsible-AI pillars, EU AI Act tiers, and your data sensitivity posture. PII handling, audit trail, and compliance framework gaps surfaced at intake.

Pipeline, Comparison & Brief

Kanban pipeline tracks each use case from intake to approval. Side-by-side comparison across up to 4 use cases. 3-page Executive Brief with Monte Carlo projections, committee deliberation summary, and platform-confirmed ROI.

Six disciplined gates — from idea to investment decision

Every use case travels the same rigorous funnel. Enterprise and LOB context flows through every gate, making each evaluation specific to your organisation — not a generic benchmark.

01

Intake 📥

Structured 5-section intake pre-loaded with your Google Cloud stack, Workspace licensing, and compliance posture. Captures org spend, data sources, and AI autonomy level — LOB context flows into every downstream gate.

02

BXT Gate 🚦

Business × Experience × Technology pre-filter aligned to Google's AI pre-filter. Weak ideas are blocked early with specific reasoning before any scoring effort is spent.

03

Feasibility Scoring 🧠

9-criterion scoring across Strategic Value · Tech Feasibility · Org Readiness. 7 platforms ranked with confidence scores, plus Monte Carlo ROI — conservative / expected / optimistic bands.

04

Platform Advisory 🧭

Google AI Decision Framework sequential gates: Gate 1 (Workspace/Gemini sufficiency) → Gate 2 (AppSheet/Agentspace low-code) → Gate 3 (Vertex AI build). Resolves to Adopt / Extend / Build tier with the Start Simple Mandate enforced.

05

Evaluation Summary 📊

Monte Carlo ROI plus all framework scores in one view — P10 / P50 / P90 confidence intervals, Google CAF, McKinsey, MIT Sloan and Gartner scores, governance checklist and risk assessment.

06

Executive Review Panel ⚖️

Four-persona live deliberation — Business Sponsor · Risk & Assurance · CIO · Committee Chair — streaming debate with canvas-style decision artifact. GADF compliance gates the GO verdict.
